# Code of conduct / 行为准则

We welcome contributors with different backgrounds and experience levels. Treat
others respectfully, discuss evidence and ideas, and give constructive feedback.
Disagreement is welcome when it remains focused on the work.

欢迎不同背景和经验的贡献者。请尊重他人，围绕证据与观点讨论，提供建设性的反馈。
意见分歧应聚焦于工作本身。

Harassment, threats, discriminatory remarks, unwanted sexual attention, deliberate
personal attacks, and publishing another person's private information without
consent are unacceptable. This applies in project issues, pull requests, discussions
and other project spaces, and when representing the project elsewhere.

本项目不接受骚扰、威胁、歧视言论、未经欢迎的性关注、蓄意人身攻击，
以及未经同意公开他人私密信息。此准则适用于项目 issue、拉取请求、讨论和其他
项目空间，也适用于在外部代表本项目的行为。

Report concerns privately to **hqysmail@gmail.com**. Include links or evidence when
available, and share only information needed to assess the report. Maintainer
**hqyyqh** may remove inappropriate content, request a correction, or restrict
participation according to the severity and repeated nature of the behavior.
Reports will be handled with care; confidentiality cannot be guaranteed when
necessary action or a legal requirement calls for disclosure. No response deadline
is promised.

如遇不当行为，请私密联系 **hqysmail@gmail.com**，尽可能提供链接或证据，
仅分享处理报告所需的信息。维护者 **hqyyqh** 可根据行为的严重程度及是否反复发生，
移除不当内容、要求纠正或限制参与。报告会得到审慎处理；必要处置或法律要求需要
披露时，无法保证绝对保密。本项目不承诺响应时限。
